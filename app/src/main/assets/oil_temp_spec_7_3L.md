# 7.3L Powerstroke Oil Temperature — Authoritative Spec

## Sweet Spot
210-220°F: Oil fully fluid, viscosity at design point, HEUI injection crisp, contaminants burned off, peak efficiency.

## Thresholds
| Range | Status | Action |
| 210-220°F | SWEET SPOT | Normal operation; best efficiency |
| < 210°F | COLD / UNDERWARMED | Moisture not burned; viscosity high; HEUI sluggish; short trips accumulate contaminants |
| 221-229°F | WARM / ACCEPTABLE | Normal driving; approaching upper range |
| 230-249°F | WARNING | Oxidation accelerating; viscosity dropping; shorten change interval |
| ≥ 250°F | CRITICAL | Accelerated breakdown; significant protection loss; change oil immediately |

## HEUI Note (7.3L-specific)
Oil temperature directly affects injection quality. Cold oil = sluggish injection response. Warm oil at 210-220°F viscosity = complete combustion = best efficiency across board.
