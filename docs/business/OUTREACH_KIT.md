**Title:** I got tired of guessing when I last changed my fuel filter, so I built an app that tracks my 7.3 by itself

> I've got a [2000 F-250 7.3]. My service records were a shoebox and my memory, and CARFAX doesn't know about anything I did in the driveway.
>
> So I built AutoSentry. Plug in an OBDLink adapter once. After that, every time the key turns and the wait-to-start light comes on, it starts recording on its own. You never open the app. It counts miles and engine hours, and it tells you when the oil, fuel filter, CAC boots, UVCH and about 20 other items are coming due, on the real Ford severe and normal schedule.
>
> What's different from Torque or FORScan:
> - It reads Ford's engine oil temp, ICP and IPR, not just generic stuff
> - It catches the crank: voltage, crank time and tries, every start
> - Trouble codes with what each one means on a 7.3 specifically
> - A service log with receipts and photos, so you can prove the work when you sell
> - When the truck doesn't answer, it says so instead of guessing
> - No account, no cloud. The app doesn't even have internet permission.
>
